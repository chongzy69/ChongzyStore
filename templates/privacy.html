Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

ChongzyStore respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ChongzyStore, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Login information
  • MLBB User ID
  • MLBB Zone ID
  • Order information
  • Payment and transaction information

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through the website may be used to provide and manage our services.

  • Creating and managing customer accounts
  • Processing diamond recharge orders
  • Displaying order history
  • Providing customer support
  • Verifying transactions
  • Improving the website and services
  • Preventing fraudulent or abusive activity

3. Account Information

When you create an account on ChongzyStore, the information you provide may be stored in our system so that we can provide account-related features and services.

4. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment services. ChongzyStore does not intentionally request or store sensitive payment credentials such as your card PIN or banking password.

Payment processing may be subject to the privacy policies and terms of the payment provider.

5. MLBB Information

When you purchase a diamond recharge, you may be required to provide your MLBB User ID and Zone ID.

This information is used for processing the requested recharge service and maintaining the associated order record.

6. Cookies and Sessions

ChongzyStore may use browser sessions and similar technologies to keep you logged in and provide necessary website functionality.

7. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

However, no online system can guarantee complete security.

8. Sharing of Information

ChongzyStore does not intend to sell customer information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to process payments, operate the website, provide requested services or comply with applicable legal requirements.

9. Customer Responsibilities

Customers should avoid sharing their ChongzyStore password with other people and should take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials.

10. Third-Party Services

ChongzyStore may use third-party services such as payment processors or communication platforms. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Children's Privacy

Users should only use ChongzyStore where permitted by applicable laws and platform requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

ChongzyStore may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact ChongzyStore through the contact method provided on the website.
