Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MLBB diamond recharge, payments, orders and account purchases on ChongzyStore.

How do I recharge MLBB Diamonds?

Select the region and diamond package you want, enter your MLBB User ID and Zone ID, provide the required information and continue to payment.

What are MLBB User ID and Zone ID?

Your MLBB User ID and Zone ID are the identifiers associated with your Mobile Legends account. They must be entered correctly when placing a recharge order.

Always check these details carefully before completing your order.

What happens after I place an order?

After creating an order, you can view its details from your account. The order will display its current status and payment information.

What payment method is available?

Available online payments are processed through the payment gateway provided during checkout.

Follow the payment instructions displayed on the order page.

How can I check my order?

Log in to your ChongzyStore account and open My Account to view your orders, order numbers, diamond packages, amounts and current order status.

Can I cancel a diamond recharge order?

Cancellation may not be possible once a digital recharge has already been successfully processed.

If you have a problem with an order, contact ChongzyStore as soon as possible.

What if I entered the wrong MLBB User ID or Zone ID?

Contact ChongzyStore as soon as possible with your order information.

Customers are responsible for checking their MLBB information before submitting an order.

How do I buy an MLBB account?

Browse the available MLBB accounts on the ChongzyStore homepage.

Select an account you are interested in and use the provided contact option to discuss the purchase directly with ChongzyStore.

Can I see the details of an MLBB account before buying?

Yes. Available account listings may include information such as rank, level, skins, heroes, description, price and screenshots.

Review the available information carefully before completing a purchase.

How do I contact ChongzyStore?

You can contact ChongzyStore directly through the WhatsApp contact option available on the website.

What if my payment was successful but my order has a problem?

Keep your order number and payment information and contact ChongzyStore with the details of the issue.

The order can then be reviewed and an appropriate resolution can be considered.

Is my account information protected?

ChongzyStore takes reasonable measures to protect customer information and uses account sessions and password protection for customer accounts.

Customers should also keep their passwords private and secure.

Still Need Help?

Our support team can help you with orders, recharge questions and account inquiries.
